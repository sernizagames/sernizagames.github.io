About the game

Step into Santa’s magical workshop and help sort the gifts before the sleigh departs! Ordinary items and enchanted objects arrive together on the conveyor belt, and it’s up to you to tell them apart and sort them correctly, all whilst following Santa’s ever-changing rules.
